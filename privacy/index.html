About this policy

This policy explains how Catch the Capybara (the “Game”) handles information. “We” means ZEHUI LIN, the developer of the Game. For privacy questions or requests, contact catchthecapybara@outlook.com.

Accounts, saves, and purchases

The Game does not require account registration or login and does not offer cloud saves. There are currently no in-app purchases. AdMob is the only advertising platform integrated into the Game.

Information and purposes

The Game uses Unreal Engine 5, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics. Game progress and preferences may be stored on your device. Analytics helps us understand use and improve the Game; Crashlytics helps us identify crashes and errors. These services process information such as app-instance or installation identifiers, device and operating-system details, app version, session and interaction events, and diagnostic reports. Crash reports can include error messages, stack traces, technical logs, and events preceding a crash. Identifiers can distinguish an installation even without a name or email address.

Advertising

AdMob provides and measures advertising and helps prevent fraud. It may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostics. We use AdMob to serve personalized ads where permitted by applicable law and your choices. On iOS, tracking across other companies’ apps and websites and access to the advertising identifier require your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Requesting ATT permission does not mean you have granted it, and ATT permission does not replace other legally required consent or advertising privacy choices. Ad personalization also depends on service settings. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing. Google and advertising partners may process data under their own policies; see How Google uses information from apps.

Support and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate problems. Information is processed by Google for the services above and by email providers when handling support. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security. External advertisements and linked services have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

On iOS, you can review tracking permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusing tracking does not automatically disable all analytics, diagnostics, or contextual advertising. Where the Game offers an advertising consent form or privacy options, use those controls to review or withdraw your choice. You can also contact us about available choices. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of your information; withdraw consent; or complain to a competent privacy authority. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and locate the relevant records. An email address alone may not identify SDK records. Uninstalling the Game does not itself delete information already held by service providers.

Legal grounds, retention, and international processing

Where required by law, we rely on consent for processing that requires it, performance of our agreement for requested services, legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the Game where permitted, and legal obligations. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described, resolving disputes, or legal requirements, then delete or de-identify it as appropriate. Our Google Analytics retention settings are 2 months for event data and 14 months for user data, with reset on new user activity enabled. New activity restarts the user-level retention period; it does not restart the event-data period. Standard aggregated reports are not subject to these settings. Other service data follows the relevant provider policies and settings. Google and email providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers must use applicable safeguards, such as approved contractual protections; contact us for details.

Children and security

The Game is not directed to children. Parents or guardians should review the Game’s age rating and supervise children’s use. If you believe a child has provided personal information without required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Updates and further information

We may update this policy when the Game or its information practices change. The date below identifies this version; material changes will be communicated as required by law. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Questions and requests: catchthecapybara@outlook.com.

Support email retention and deletion

We keep support emails and attachments while handling your inquiry and for up to 12 months after it is closed, to follow up on the issue. We delete them earlier when no longer needed or when a verified deletion request is granted. If applicable law requires further retention, or retention is lawfully necessary for a specific dispute, we keep only the necessary information for that purpose and delete it when that need ends. To request deletion, email catchthecapybara@outlook.com, preferably from the address used for your inquiry, with “Delete Support Emails” in the subject and the approximate date or subject of the correspondence. This subject line is helpful but not required. We may ask for limited information to verify your request; do not send identity documents unless specifically needed and requested. We aim to reply and complete eligible deletions within 30 calendar days of receiving the request, or sooner where law requires. If verification, a lawful extension, or a retention exception applies, we will explain the status, reason, and expected timing within the applicable deadline. Deletion covers the relevant messages and attachments in mail folders and copies under our control. Provider-managed backups and recovery copies follow the provider’s applicable retention and deletion processes and may not disappear immediately. Deleting support emails does not automatically delete Analytics or Crashlytics records; please identify any broader privacy request separately.

처리하는 정보와 목적

Analytics는 이용 현황 파악과 게임 개선에, Crashlytics는 비정상 종료와 오류 조사에 사용됩니다. 이 서비스들은 앱 인스턴스 또는 설치 식별자, 기기 및 운영체제 정보, 앱 버전, 세션과 상호작용 이벤트, 진단 보고서 등을 처리합니다. 비정상 종료 보고서에는 오류 메시지, 스택 추적, 기술 로그, 종료 직전 이벤트가 포함될 수 있습니다. 이름이나 이메일 주소가 없어도 식별자로 특정 설치를 구별할 수 있습니다.

광고

AdMob은 광고 제공, 효과 측정 및 부정행위 방지에 사용됩니다. 법률상 필요한 경우 동의가 필요한 처리에는 동의, 요청한 서비스에는 계약 이행, 허용되는 범위의 게임 유지 및 보안에는 정당한 이익, 그 밖의 필요한 처리에는 법적 의무를 근거로 합니다. 설명된 목적, 분쟁 해결 또는 법적 의무에 필요한 기간만 정보를 보유한 뒤 적절히 삭제하거나 비식별화합니다. 關於本政策

本政策說明 Catch the Capybara（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理資訊。「我們」指本遊戲的開發者 ZEHUI LIN。隱私相關問題或請求，請聯絡 catchthecapybara@outlook.com。

帳號、存檔與購買

本遊戲沒有帳號註冊、登入或雲端存檔功能。目前不提供 App 內購買，整合的廣告平台僅有 AdMob。

資訊類型與用途

本遊戲使用 Unreal Engine 5、Google AdMob、Google Analytics for Firebase 及 Firebase Crashlytics。遊戲進度及偏好設定可能儲存在您的裝置上。Analytics 協助我們了解使用情況及改善遊戲；Crashlytics 用於調查當機及錯誤。這些服務會處理應用程式執行個體或安裝識別碼、裝置與作業系統資訊、應用程式版本、工作階段與互動事件，以及診斷報告等資訊。當機報告可能包含錯誤訊息、堆疊追蹤、技術紀錄及當機前的事件。即使沒有姓名或電子郵件地址，識別碼仍可能用於區分個別安裝。

廣告

AdMob 用於提供及衡量廣告，並協助防範詐欺。它可能處理 IP 位址、由 IP 位址推估的概略位置、裝置或廣告識別碼、廣告曝光與互動，以及診斷資訊。我們在適用法律及您的選擇允許的範圍內，使用 AdMob 提供個人化廣告。在 iOS 上，跨其他公司的應用程式與網站追蹤，或存取廣告識別碼，須取得您的 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）授權。提出 ATT 請求不代表您已授權，ATT 授權也不取代法律要求的其他同意或廣告隱私選擇。廣告個人化也受服務設定影響。非個人化廣告仍可能涉及資料處理。Google 及廣告合作夥伴可能依其各自政策處理資訊，請參閱 Google 如何使用來自應用程式的資訊。
