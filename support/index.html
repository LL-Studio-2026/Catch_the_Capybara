Contact the developer

Need help with Catch the Capybara? Contact ZEHUI LIN at catchthecapybara@outlook.com. You can report bugs, missing ad rewards, progress issues, or privacy concerns without creating a support account. You may write in English, Japanese, Korean, or Traditional Chinese. Response time depends on the nature and volume of requests.

What to include

Please include the app version, device model, iOS version, approximate time and time zone of the issue, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. A screenshot or short recording can help; remove private information first. For an ad issue, include when you watched the ad and whether a completion message appeared. Never send passwords, Apple Account credentials, or full payment-card details.

The Game crashes or will not open

Try closing and reopening the Game, restarting your device, updating the Game and iOS, and checking available storage. If it continues, email the details above. Contact us before deleting or reinstalling if you need to preserve progress: locally saved data may be lost.

An ad is unavailable or a reward is missing

Check your connection and try again later. Ads may be unavailable in some regions or at some times. Follow the displayed reward conditions and wait for completion. If a reward does not arrive, report the time and what you saw. Do not change privacy choices merely to troubleshoot an ad.

Accounts, saves, and purchases

The Game does not require account registration or login and does not offer cloud saves. There are currently no in-app purchases. AdMob is the only advertising platform integrated into the Game.

For missing progress, tell us whether you changed devices, reinstalled, or deleted app data. The Game has no cloud-save feature, so progress is not synchronized between devices through a game account. Recovery of local progress cannot be guaranteed. Check before deleting or reinstalling the Game if you need to preserve progress.

Privacy requests

Email us with “Privacy Request” in the subject and describe the request, such as access, correction, deletion, or a question about consent. Send only the information needed; we may ask for limited additional details to verify and locate relevant records. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Support email retention and deletion

We keep support emails and attachments while handling your inquiry and for up to 12 months after it is closed, to follow up on the issue. We delete them earlier when no longer needed or when a verified deletion request is granted. If applicable law requires further retention, or retention is lawfully necessary for a specific dispute, we keep only the necessary information for that purpose and delete it when that need ends. To request deletion, email catchthecapybara@outlook.com, preferably from the address used for your inquiry, with “Delete Support Emails” in the subject and the approximate date or subject of the correspondence. This subject line is helpful but not required. We may ask for limited information to verify your request; do not send identity documents unless specifically needed and requested. We aim to reply and complete eligible deletions within 30 calendar days of receiving the request, or sooner where law requires. If verification, a lawful extension, or a retention exception applies, we will explain the status, reason, and expected timing within the applicable deadline. Deletion covers the relevant messages and attachments in mail folders and copies under our control. Provider-managed backups and recovery copies follow the provider’s applicable retention and deletion processes and may not disappear immediately. Deleting support emails does not automatically delete Analytics or Crashlytics records; please identify any broader privacy request separately.
